# Dr Plumbing website

The website helps homeowners understand available services, see real project work, and book a visit or contact the business.

## Service and city pages

Present complete bathroom remodeling as a managed project, not a limited menu of plumbing fixtures. The owner confirmed framing, electrical, plumbing, fixture installation, complete kitchen remodeling, rain showers and steam showers. Do not add licensing claims or unconfirmed equipment features.

Use owner-supplied project photos. Captions explain a relevant feature or stage of work in plain language. Omit decorative details and trade jargon. Preserve existing services and URLs.

Photo alt text, captions and surrounding copy identify the service shown and help customers find and understand the services we provide. Write for customers and SEO/AEO, not object inventories. Include the business name where useful. Every service photo on a city-specific page appends that page's city, including hero, section and gallery photos. General pages keep the same service description without a city suffix. This suffix identifies the service area, not a photographed job location. Keep descriptions accurate and avoid unsupported claims or keyword lists. Confirm a new alt-text direction with Jide before applying it.

City pages reuse the Mercer Island service hierarchy, copy and photography. Localize city names, verified landmarks and navigation destinations. Shared service and photo records keep the pages consistent.

The [surface contract](.impeccable/surfaces/src-sitecontent-tsx.md) records the city scope, page structure, photo corrections and finish review. This local extension retains the incumbent visual design. No deployment is authorized.
