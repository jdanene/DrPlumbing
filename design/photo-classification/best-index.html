Visual selection rules

Palette: Neutral whites, slate equipment, natural copper and wood. Keep real blue equipment and outdoor greens; do not tint them to the brand.

Light: Prefer readable, evenly lit subjects. Keep genuine work lighting where the task remains clear.

Tone: Real projects, understandable equipment and approachable people. Friendly work portraits are useful here, despite the skill's stock-photo rule against eye contact.

Composition: Rank the clearest service view first. Add a useful detail or work view, rather than three nearly identical frames. Favor finished residential fixtures where available.

Texture: Keep the existing clean JPEG copies. Add no grain, filters or simulated detail.

Treatment: No crop or color grade applied. Crop notes and focal points guide a later website placement; they are not permission to remove equipment or alter the work.

Selection scope: Up to three distinct, useful options per reviewed category. These are ranked alternatives, not three mandatory slots on the website. Uncertain classifications stay excluded.
