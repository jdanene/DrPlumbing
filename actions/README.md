# Actions

The record of every optimization made to Dr Plumbing's presence outside this
codebase: the Google Business Profile, directories, reports to Google, and the
scans that measure them. One folder per day, named by the date; inside it,
`action.md` says what we did, why, and what we are looking for, and `evidence/`
holds the data, reports and screenshots that justified the change.

Why a record: MapPack measures rank by rescanning. A rescan only explains a rank
move if we know what changed, when, and what we expected. This folder is that log.
The marks on the MapPack Website tab ("Mark a change") point here.

Rules:

- One change per rescan where possible, so the scan shows which change moved the rank.
- Record the exact text entered and the previous value, so a rollback is a copy and paste.
- Business facts come from Jidè. Record who supplied each fact and the date.
- All relevant evidence goes in the day's `evidence/` folder, not in chat or the scratchpad.
- Never record secrets, passwords or account recovery details.
