A working log for a two-hour session that starts at about 2:30 PM on October 25, 2026. Entries are added as each step happens, newest last, with the exact values entered. Nothing secret is recorded here: no passwords, tax IDs, bank details or policy numbers.

## Why

Utility programs let a plumber's customers get rebates through him and put him on a list that customers, Google and AI assistants read. PSE's Trade Ally network is the only one with a referral path: after performance requirements, a member can become a Recommended Energy Professional and receive PSE customer referrals. Membership is free; review takes two to three weeks.

Evidence: [utility-programs.html](evidence/utility-programs.html), the ranked action list researched the same day from PSE's, Seattle City Light's and the other utilities' own pages. The idea came from a ChatGPT conversation Mehran had on October 24 about "contracts with PSE"; the research confirmed the Trade Ally route and found that none of these programs hands out paid jobs.

## What we are looking for

- PSE approves the application, assigns training and sends a Participation Agreement, two to three weeks after submission.
- Dr Plumbing appears in PSE's contractor resources for Water Heating and Space Heating & Cooling.
- Later: Recommended Energy Professional status and the first PSE referral, logged as the "utility referral" lead source in the owner app.

### 2. PSE's portal read, before applying

Opened pse.com/rebates/trade-allies, then "APPLY NOW", which leads to PSE's Trade Ally Portal (psetradeally.my.site.com). Read its application overview, Performance Criteria and Program Guidelines. What they add to the earlier research:

- **Licenses must be active for 12 months or more.** The portal lists "Active WA State business and contractor licenses (12+ months)". Dr Plumbing's L&I record is DRPLUPH740ND; how long it has been active under that number is the first fact to confirm.
- **Apply for Water Heating, not Space Heating and Cooling.** The Space Heating and Cooling program requires an electrical license under state law; Water Heating requires the plumbing license, which Dr Plumbing holds. "Other Residential Services" (earthquake shutoff valves, gas piping) can be added beside Water Heating; it carries no rebates today.
- **Job counts per six-month cycle.** Water Heating needs 2 jobs per cycle to stay a Trade Ally and 5 to qualify as a Recommended Energy Professional, the tier that receives PSE referrals. Cycles run March to August and September to February. Non-rebated jobs can be submitted to count.
- **Upkeep.** Log into the portal at least once every 90 days; complete annual program training; keep license and insurance documents current; resolve any complaint or verification issue within 30 days.
- **Rebate paperwork.** Documentation for an instant rebate is due within 30 days of installation; PSE reimburses within 45 days of complete paperwork.
- **Timeline.** Three to eight weeks in all: verification and training assignments in weeks 1 and 2, trainings due within two weeks of assignment, the agreement in weeks 3 and 4.

Next: the portal needs an account before the application. Creating it is Mehran's or Jidè's step, with the business email and a password only they hold; the application itself follows.
