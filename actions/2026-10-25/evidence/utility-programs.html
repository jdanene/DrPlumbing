Utility programs do two things for a plumber: they let his customers get rebates through him, and they put him on a list that customers, Google and AI assistants read. None of them hands out jobs. What it gives
Instant rebates he can offer customers on qualifying heat pump water heaters and heating; a free listing; and, after meeting performance requirements, "Recommended Energy Professional" status with PSE-generated customer referrals and PSE co-branding. That referral tier is the one lead source on this page.
Requirements, from PSE's page
Active Washington business and contractor licenses plus the plumbing specialty license; workers' compensation with at least $1,000,000 coverage for employees on PSE projects; general liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, umbrella allowed; auto liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence; industry memberships or certifications per trade. More applies to give instant rebates and to become a Recommended Energy Professional.
How
Enrollment application on PSE's Trade Ally Portal: company information, copies of licenses and insurance, products and territory, vendor account and ACH forms, a W-9. Review takes two to three weeks, then assigned training, then a Participation Agreement to sign. Questions: a monthly open call on the third Friday, or tradeallysupport@pse.com.
Cost
Free.
