# One indexed site earns the AI shortlist

Research report for Dr Plumbing & Heating, Newcastle, WA. All source notes were read on 9 October 2026. Each claim carries a standing label: **documented** (a provider's own page), **measured** (a study with a stated sample and date), **reported** (a news outlet and date), **practitioner** (a named person or forum; "sells" marks one who sells a related service), or **observed** (a researcher's own test with date). Where the notes record a gap or an unfetchable source, one line says so.

## 1. Answer first

Effective AEO for this plumber is ordinary local presence, built so that a search index can retrieve it. AEO (answer engine optimization) is the work of getting a business named or cited when a person asks an AI assistant whom to hire. The assistants answer by live retrieval: they search the web at answer time, shortlist firms from directories, review sites and Google Business Profile records for the named city, then cite the shortlisted firms' own pages (documented: [Google AI features page, updated 10 December 2025](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features); measured: [BrightLocal, 60,970 checks, 26 August 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/); [8ight.AI, 1,630 API answers, 5 October 2026](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-names-3-4-of-tested-local-firms-with-under-25-reviews-8ight-finds/)). The work is therefore: a verified Google Business Profile with the address hidden and the served cities listed; claimed Yelp, Bing Places and BBB (Better Business Bureau) profiles and a Washington L&I (Department of Labor & Industries) contractor record under the exact business name; one indexed page per service and per served city that answers the question in its first three sentences; and a review count past 20, then past 100, with reviews that name the service and the city. Nothing built only for machines has measured support: llms.txt showed no relationship with citation across about 300,000 domains (measured: [SE Ranking, 2026](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/)), schema markup showed no gain in the one controlled test (measured: [Ahrefs, 1,885 pages, 11 May 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/)), and the GEO paper's content edits raised visibility only inside a simulator (peer-reviewed: [Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735); preprint survey: [Martinez, 15 July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14035v1)).

One website, not two. Every assistant's eligibility rule is ordinary indexing (documented: the crawler pages in Section 11). A second domain starts with no links, history or Business Profile, duplicates the first site's service claims, and matches Google's doorway definition (documented: [Google spam policies, updated 28 August 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). Clock Tower X, ten domains and about 900 pages on a reported $46.5 million a year, produced no ChatGPT citations in Axios's test (reported: [Quincy Institute, 24 July 2026](https://quincyinst.org/research/the-eighth-front-inside-israels-1-billion-influence-campaign/)). The machine layer belongs on the existing domain; Section 5 lists its contents.

## 2. How assistants build an answer

For "who should I hire" questions, every major assistant answers from live retrieval through a search index, not from training memory. Training data is the text a model learned from before release, and no page owner can verify inclusion. Retrieval is a web search run at answer time; grounding feeds the retrieved pages to the model; a citation is the link the answer shows for a claim. The vendors document the split in their crawler names: GPTBot trains while OAI-SearchBot indexes for ChatGPT search (documented: [OpenAI crawler page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)); ClaudeBot trains while Claude-SearchBot indexes and Claude-User fetches on request (documented: [Claude Help Center, updated 7 April 2026](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler)); PerplexityBot indexes for Perplexity (documented: [Perplexity docs](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots)); Googlebot serves Search, including AI Overviews and AI Mode, while Google-Extended governs only Gemini training and grounding and "does not impact a site's inclusion in Google Search" (documented: [Google common crawlers, updated 14 July 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/google-common-crawlers)). Blocking a training crawler does not remove a site from search; blocking a search crawler does. The training route is closed to a small site: Common Crawl, the public archive behind about 80% of GPT-3's tokens (reported: [Drop Site, 28 July 2026](https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/israel-brad-parscale-ai-chatbots-gaza)), archives "a randomly selected subset" of a site and takes no inclusion requests (documented: [Common Crawl FAQ](https://commoncrawl.org/faq)).

Each assistant uses a different index. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode run on the Google Search index with query fan-out, which means the system issues several related searches per question and merges the results; a page needs only to be indexed and snippet-eligible (documented: Google AI features page). Copilot is "grounded in the web" through Bing (documented: [Microsoft Learn FAQ, updated 18 August 2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/faq)). ChatGPT's use of Bing is reported, not fetched; OpenAI's statement returned 403 (gap), and the overlap between ChatGPT citations and Bing's top 10 fell from 87% in late 2024 (measured: Seer Interactive, via [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-search-rankings-llm-mentions-450348)) to 8.10% in July 2025 (measured: [Ahrefs, 15,000 prompts, 11 August 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/)). ChatGPT also holds licensed Yelp data, 330 million reviews and 8 million listings (reported: [Axios, 23 July 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/23/yelp-reviews-chatgpt-geo-partnership), via secondary coverage). Perplexity runs its own index and has licensed Yelp since March 2024 (reported: [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/perplexity-enhances-ai-search-engine-with-direct-yelp-data-integration/)). Claude's provider was reported as Brave Search in March 2025 (reported: [TechCrunch via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-appears-using-brave-power-170703042.html)); Anthropic now documents its own Claude-SearchBot, and its subprocessor list could not be fetched (gap).

Location settings are thin: Perplexity documents a Location setting that falls back to "your network location" (documented: [Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352990-account-settings)), Google Search personalizes on "a general location you've searched from in the past" (documented: [Google Search Help](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/17026260?hl=en), via snippet), and no fetchable page describes one for ChatGPT, consumer Copilot, the Gemini app or Claude (gap). The measurements decide the matter: all 447 ChatGPT business cards in one study matched the city named in the prompt, not the searcher's location (measured: [Spearleaf, 216 markets, 25 September 2026](https://spearleaf.com/research/how-chatgpt-picks-a-local-business/)), and 90% of ChatGPT's named businesses fell within 287 km of the test point against 64 km for AI Mode and 38 km for AI Overviews (measured: [BrightLocal, 200,085 searches, 16 September 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)). For ChatGPT the city in the text sets the place; for Google's surfaces the device does.

Who gets cited follows from two stages. First the page must be retrieved, which means ranking somewhere for the question or a fan-out variant: in 1.4 million ChatGPT prompts, 49.98% of retrieved URLs were cited, and cited pages had a title-to-prompt cosine similarity of 0.602 against 0.484 (measured: [Ahrefs, 15 April 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/why-chatgpt-cites-pages/)); only 38% of AI Overview citations ranked in the top 10, and 31.0% ranked beyond position 100 (measured: Ahrefs, 863,000 keywords, via [Search Engine Journal, 2 March 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overview-citations-from-top-ranking-pages-drop-sharply/568637/)). Second, the model selects among retrieved pages, and breadth of mention counts: across 75,000 brands, branded web mentions correlated 0.664 with AI Overview brand presence while backlinks correlated 0.218 (measured: [Ahrefs, 26 May 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)). Two facts bound the whole: ChatGPT's app searched in only about 42% of runs, so 57.8% of answers carried no citation (measured: [Schulte et al., arXiv 2604.07585, 8 April 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07585v1)), and for pure local intent Google's AI Overview appeared on 15% of queries while the local pack appeared on 93%, with "service plus city" questions triggering an AI Overview 97% of the time (measured: [Whitespark, 540 queries, 12 May 2025](https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/); the 15%/93% split is from secondary summaries because the original could not be opened).

Three notes on the nulls. The schema test is the only difference-in-differences design in this literature: it compares the change in citations for pages that added JSON-LD against the change for matched pages that did not, which removes trends shared by both groups; its −4.6% on AI Overviews had odds of chance near 1 in 2,500, but the pages already held 100-plus citations each, so it says nothing about a small site (measured: [Ahrefs, 11 May 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/)). The llms.txt result holds across hostile and friendly parties: Mueller wrote "none of the AI services have said they're using LLMs.TXT" (practitioner: via [Search Engine Journal, 17 April 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-says-llms-txt-comparable-to-keywords-meta-tag/544804/)), and its author defends it only for coding agents he configures himself (practitioner: Jeremy Howard on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47058870)). The GEO paper's winning edits included model-invented statistics, and the 45-study survey found "no reviewed technique shows a stable, longitudinal, cross-platform causal effect on organic discoverability" (preprint: [Martinez, 15 July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14035v1)). Specifics help a page that is already retrieved and losing; they do not get it retrieved.

## 5. One website or two

One domain. Keep the existing site and make it the machine layer. The machine-facing second site fails four tests.

Clock Tower X is the test case. Under a contract disclosed through FARA (the Foreign Agents Registration Act) in September 2025, the firm built ten to eleven single-topic .org sites with about 900 short, bullet-pointed, heavily sourced pages, each footer disclosing distribution "on behalf of the State of Israel," and bought monthly SEO from Market Brew (reported: [Washington Examiner, 25 March 2026](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/investigations/4501168/israel-funds-front-websites-push-chatgpt-promote-pro-war-messaging/); [Drop Site, 28 July 2026](https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/israel-brad-parscale-ai-chatbots-gaza)). Filings put the fee at $6 million for four months, then $9 million, then $46.5 million over one year (reported: [Quincy Institute, 24 July 2026](https://quincyinst.org/research/the-eighth-front-inside-israels-1-billion-influence-campaign/); the FARA exhibits could not be retrieved). Common Crawl captured the sites 912 times between 1 January and 10 June 2026; Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini cited them in live answers; Axios found no citations when it searched ChatGPT with phrases from the sites; Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini flagged the sponsorship from the footer; the sites drew "a few hundred" visitors a month; and an Israeli official told Time the situation had "only gotten worse" (reported: Drop Site; [Time, 13 July 2026](https://time.com/article/2026/07/13/brad-parscale-israel-influence-operation/)). Market Brew's own CTO told Axios that direct influence over AI systems is "a misconception" (reported: Axios, 25 April 2026, via Quincy; Axios returned 403).

Google's spam policy names the pattern. The policy, updated 28 August 2026, counts "attempting to manipulate generative AI responses in Google Search" as spam and defines doorway abuse as "sites or pages... created to rank for specific, similar search queries" (documented: [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies); full wording in Section 9). A second plumber domain targeting the same service-plus-city queries is a doorway by that definition; Mueller said Google "may decide to display content from just one site out of the collection, or it might demote all of them," and advised "one strong website" with location pages (practitioner: John Mueller, via [Search Engine Journal, 2018](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-multiple-sites-ip-not-problem-similar-content/254799/) and [Local Search Forum](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/business-with-2-locations-one-website-or-two-websites-whats-the-best-approach.45668/post-99009)). Authority does not transfer: position 1 was cited 24.9% of the time and position 10 10.2% (measured: [Conductor, 167,867,680 citations, 25 September 2026](https://www.conductor.com/academy/aio-vs-organic-rankings/)), and a new domain has no links, history or Business Profile. Self-description is discounted: Mueller said an LLM cannot trust a site's own description, since every site would claim to be "the best website ever" (practitioner: via [Search Engine Journal, 15 June 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-mueller-says-llms-txt-cant-help-llms-differentiate-sites/579304/)), and three of five assistants surfaced Clock Tower X's affiliation from a footer line.

The recommended shape is one domain whose pages serve people and machines at once. Its machine layer contains exactly seven parts. First, one page per service and per served city, opening with a two- or three-sentence direct answer (what the firm does, where, how it prices, how fast it responds), then a visible plain-language FAQ and dates on anything that changes, with name, phone, hours, license number and services in the server-rendered HTML, because the OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity crawlers fetch JavaScript but do not execute it (measured: [Vercel and MERJ, December 2024](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler); no newer independent test exists). Second, identical name, phone, hours and service-area cities on the site, the Google Business Profile and every directory. Third, LocalBusiness or Plumber JSON-LD with telephone, openingHoursSpecification, priceRange, areaServed and url that mirrors the visible text; a hidden-address business must not put an address in markup that the page does not show (documented: [Google LocalBusiness, updated 8 September 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business); Google AI features page). FAQPage markup is inert since Google withdrew FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026 (reported: [Search Engine Journal, 10 May 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-drops-faq-rich-results-from-search/574429/)). Fourth, a robots.txt that allows Googlebot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User and PerplexityBot; GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended govern training and are a separate choice. Fifth, an XML sitemap declared in robots.txt, which CCBot also reads (documented: Common Crawl FAQ). Sixth, no edge block: Cloudflare has blocked AI crawlers by default on new zones since 1 July 2025 (reported: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/695501/cloudflare-block-ai-crawlers-default)), and its free-plan Bot Fight Mode served Googlebot a noindex challenge page that cut one site's impressions from 1,153 to 16 (practitioner: [Serdarcan Buyukdereli, 13 August 2026](https://daily.dev/posts/how-cloudflare-bot-fight-mode-quietly-killed-our-google-rankings-lbdhmurms)). Seventh, Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools verified. An llms.txt file is optional and has no expected effect.

## 6. Measurement

Measure presence rate from a frozen prompt set run many times, and test the machine-layer theory as a difference-in-differences with a treated arm and a control arm. One run of one prompt is not a measurement: the per-brand detection rate from a single run has a standard error of 0.370, falling to 0.081 at 7 runs and 0.041 at 9 (measured: [Schulte et al., arXiv 2604.07585, 32 prompts, 4 engines, 8 April 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07585v1)). Two runs of the same prompt returned the same brand list less than 1 time in 100 across 2,961 responses (measured: [SparkToro with Gumshoe, 30 January 2026](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/), via extract); repeat overlap on local prompts was 23% for ChatGPT, 21% for AI Mode and 33% for AI Overviews (measured: BrightLocal, 16 September 2026); cited sources turn over about 65% day to day, a Jaccard overlap of 0.336 to 0.423, where Jaccard is the share of items two sets have in common (measured: Schulte et al.).

The question set is 40 to 60 prompts in customer language, frozen before the baseline, with equal counts per intent in each arm: emergency ("burst pipe in Newcastle WA, who can come today"), service-specific (water heater, drain, sewer line, repipe), evaluative ("best plumber in Newcastle, Washington"), "near me" with location set and no city in the text, comparison of two named firms, and price. Mix short list-style and conversational forms, since concise prompts produced up to 20% more brand mentions (measured: Peec AI, 1,754 prompts, via Let's Data Science). Rewriting a prompt mid-test restarts its series. The four APIs take these parameters (documented on the provider pages read 9 October 2026); the notes give Newcastle's coordinates as about 47.53, −122.16 from general knowledge, to verify before use.

Run two context conditions and report them separately: blank (one user turn, location fields only) and primed (a prior turn stating "I'm a homeowner in Newcastle, WA, and my water heater is leaking"); no measurement of the gap between them exists (gap). Log model ID, tool version, fingerprint, location payload, full answer, sources and citations per run, and count rate-limit errors, since Claude returns `too_many_requests` inside an HTTP 200.

Metrics per engine per week: mention rate (the business named anywhere in the answer), own-domain citation rate, share of voice against a fixed list of the nine competitors in Section 4 plus Bee's Plumbing, and top-three inclusion. The prompt is the unit of inference because runs of one prompt are correlated (practitioner: [Parse.gl](https://www.parse.gl/blog/ai-visibility-drop-real-or-noise), sells tracking): compute each prompt's weekly rate, average across prompts, and bootstrap across prompts for the interval (a bootstrap resamples the prompts with replacement), with the pooled binomial interval as a floor. Cadence: 7 runs per prompt per engine per day in each condition, or 3 per day over 4 weeks if budget forces it (28 daily single runs reach a standard error of 0.033); a 2-to-4-week baseline, one deployment day, 4 weeks post.

The difference-in-differences test compares the before-to-after change in the treated arm with the before-to-after change in a control arm, so model updates, index churn and seasonal demand, which hit both arms, cancel out. Option A assigns by service: apply the machine layer to the water-heater pages, keep the drain-cleaning pages unchanged, and split prompts by service. Option B assigns by city: treat the Newcastle pages, keep Renton or Issaquah pages as they are, and set `user_location` per arm. Option B is cleaner when the city pages are otherwise identical; Option A avoids the unverified location emulation. The estimand is (post − pre) treated minus (post − pre) control, per engine. Pre-register prompts, arms, metrics, windows and the decision rule, for example "mention rate up at least 10 points with a 95% bootstrap interval excluding zero on the primary engine." Expect a null on Google's surfaces, since Google states no machine-readable files are needed.

Sample sizes follow from the two-proportion test at α 0.05 and power 0.8: a move from 10% to 20% needs about 199 answers per arm per period, and 20% to 30% about 293, matching the 294 a vendor computed (practitioner: [Cloro](https://cloro.dev/blog/ai-visibility-sample-size/), sells tracking; the 199 is the note author's calculation). Because of clustering by prompt, plan at least two weeks per period, about 2,900 answers per arm at 30 prompts and 7 runs. Confounders to log: model or backend changes; the 65% daily source churn, which means citation metrics need longer windows than mention metrics; competitor profile, review and page changes; plumbing seasonality; source collapses such as Reddit's fall from 41.7% to 0.0% of ChatGPT citations in one month; and silent sample loss to rate limits (37.5% of one author's scheduled checks failed on quota: measured, 8ight.AI).

An API answer and a person's app session are different instruments. The API gives a memoryless session with a declared location, a forced search and a pinned model, which suits before-versus-after and treated-versus-control. The app adds a hidden system prompt (Claude's "does not apply to the Claude API": documented, Claude release notes), memory, device location, personalization, the product's own decision whether to search, and surfaces the API does not expose (AI Mode and AI Overviews "may use different models and techniques" from the Gemini API: documented, Google AI features page). The source mix differs: Yelp appeared in 0% of ChatGPT API answers and about 80% of app answers in two studies weeks apart (measured: 8ight.AI; BrightLocal). Read API rates as relative measures, not as the share of Newcastle residents who would see the business; bridge the gap with a monthly manual panel in fresh logged-out sessions on a device in the service area and the two free reports in Section 7.

## 9. Lines not to cross

Google's rules name the shortcuts, Reddit enforces its own, and three of five assistants surface a source's affiliation. Honest content costs less than planted content because planted content is the kind that gets removed.

Google's spam policies, last updated 28 August 2026, state that spam can include "attempting to manipulate generative AI responses in Google Search," that scaled content abuse covers "using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without adding value for users" and applies "no matter how it's created," and that doorway abuse covers pages "created to rank for specific, similar search queries" (documented: [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). The helpful-content page, updated 5 October 2026, asks whether automation is "self-evident to visitors through disclosures" (documented: [Google helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)), and Google's AI optimization guide says to "ignore tactics like 'chunking' content, creating unnecessary AI text files (like llms.txt), or pursuing inauthentic mentions" (documented: [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)).
Four rules follow from the evidence. Do not fake an in-city address with a virtual office or mailbox; the study that found the address effect warns of suspension (measured: Spearleaf, 25 September 2026). Do not change publish dates without substantive edits; faked freshness "can erode trust and may be detected" (practitioner: [Ahrefs, 13 March 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-to-rank-on-chatgpt), sells SEO tools). Do not invent statistics or quotations; the GEO paper's winning edits "relied on prompting the model to invent statistics and quotes" (practitioner: [Joseph Timpson](https://josephtimpson.com/insights/generative-engine-optimization-research)), and Google now names manipulation of AI responses as spam. Do not serve one page to bots and another to people; Mueller called Markdown-for-bots "a stupid idea" (practitioner: via [Search Engine Journal, 4 February 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-mueller-calls-markdown-for-bots-idea-a-stupid-idea/566598/)), and the pattern is cloaking. The notes contain no text of Google's or Yelp's review-solicitation policies; read those pages before the review program starts (gap).

On seeded mentions, the one measured experiment is the warning: Otterly ran four owned subreddits with injected votes and comments for 60 days, Reddit removed all four around 22 June 2026 under Content Policy Rule 2, content manipulation, and 3,740 AI citations went to zero (practitioner: [Otterly, updated 1 October 2026](https://otterly.ai/blog/reddit-ban-geo-experiment/), sells tracking). Assistants also read affiliations: Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini flagged Paxpoint.org as sponsored by the Israeli government from its FARA footer; Perplexity and Copilot did not (reported: Drop Site, 28 July 2026). Honest content wins on mechanism: a page that states what the firm does, where, on what price basis and under which license is one that a directory, a review site and an assistant can verify against each other, and cross-verification is how every assistant builds a shortlist.

## 10. What this means for MapPack

MapPack should treat an AI answer as one more surface in its measurement model, record it per run, and attach the no-cost actions below to the plumber's campaign board.

The AEO surface records, per run: the question (prompt ID, text, intent, blank or primed); the city (the declared location payload and the city named in the text, kept apart because ChatGPT follows the text and Google follows the device); the assistant (vendor, app or API, model ID, tool version, fingerprint, timestamp); named or not (mention, top-three inclusion); who instead (every business named, in order, resolved to the fixed competitor list); and sources cited (every URL, classed as Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, license registry, directory, best-of list, own site or other). The weekly aggregate is presence rate per prompt with a bootstrap interval, share of voice, own-domain citation rate, and a source-gap table of the domains this assistant cites for this trade in this city and whether the business appears on each.

The actions it adds fall into five groups, each proven by a public page: profile actions (Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, BBB, the L&I record); listing actions (Angi, Expertise.com, ConsumerAffairs, Yelp's organic top-10 per served city); page actions (one answer-first page per service and per city); review actions (ask each customer to name the service and the city, reply to each, one new review every two weeks); and access actions (crawler and edge checks). Each action ends with a re-sample, under the existing rule that only verified delivery starts observation.

The first steps cost nothing. Three facts about the deployed site come from the repository (observed: `DrPlumbing/scripts/prerender.mjs` and `src/`, 9 October 2026): the build prerenders every public route to static HTML, writes a robots.txt that allows all agents and declares a sitemap, and ships one Areas page listing cities rather than a page per city.

1. Test crawler access from outside: fetch the homepage and one service page with the user-agent strings of Googlebot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, ClaudeBot, GPTBot and PerplexityBot, and confirm HTTP 200 with page text in the body.
